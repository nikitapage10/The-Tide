*Children of the Core*

The **Obscarron** are a people born from the earth itself, their origins tied to a cataclysmic event that reshaped both their bodies and their destiny. Buried beneath molten rock and ash during the **Undertow**, their ancestors were trapped and transformed by the planet’s geothermal forces. Over time, their bodies fused with volcanic stone, metals, and obsidian, giving them dark, stone-like features and a deep connection to the planet’s **core**. This event, known to the Obscarron as the **Shatterbirth**, marked the beginning of their civilization, as they rose from the depths of the earth, believing they had been reborn to serve as guardians of the planet’s molten heart.

The Obscarron built their society around the flow of **geothermal energy**, constructing magnificent **Molten Cities** powered by lava and molten metal. These cities, alive with the constant flow of Earthblood, reflected their reverence for the core and its power. Culturally, they adopted rituals and symbols from ancient ruins they uncovered—remnants of an **Aztec-like civilization**—though they had no knowledge of the true origins of these practices. Their religious beliefs were deeply tied to the planet, with the **Elders of the Core** overseeing rituals like the **Ashen Offerings**, where precious stones and metals were cast into the lava as a tribute to the core’s energy.

For centuries, the Obscarron lived in harmony with the molten forces beneath them, their society united by their reverence for the core and the belief that they were its chosen stewards. However, the cataclysmic events of the **Tide** brought massive upheaval. The Drowning, which flooded and solidified their once-flowing lava rivers, triggered what the Obscarron call the **Great Petrification**, a disaster that brought their cities to a halt. With the earth’s molten lifeblood no longer flowing, their cities stagnated, leaving the Obscarron in disarray and struggling to restore what they had lost.

In the wake of this disaster, a new ruling class emerged—the **Geomagnus**. These individuals, who developed powerful control over **magnetic fields** and **geothermal energy** during the Tide’s aftermath, quickly took charge of the rebuilding process. Their mastery of the earth’s forces made them essential to the restoration of the Molten Cities, but their authoritarian rule also created deep divisions within Obscarron society. The Geomagnus imposed strict control over the reconstruction efforts, consolidating power and enforcing their vision of rebuilding at any cost, often at the expense of the rest of the population.

Today, the Obscarron are a people divided. While the **Geomagnus** continue to consolidate their rule, using their powers to restore the flow of Earthblood and rebuild their cities, the majority of the Obscarron remain under their control, with many beginning to resist this new order. **Resistance movements** have formed, opposing the authoritarian rule of the Geomagnus and seeking to restore the balance and equality their society once held dear. As they face both internal strife and the lasting effects of the Tide, the Obscarron stand at a crossroads, with the future of their civilization hanging in the balance.

## Origins and Early Evolution

During the **Undertow**, violent volcanic eruptions tore through the regions surrounding what was once Latin America. The skies darkened as ash and molten rock spewed across the land, burying entire cities beneath layers of molten lava and debris. What should have been the end for these people, however, became something else entirely.

The **energies released by the Undertow**, combined with the extreme volcanic heat, created a unique, transformative environment. As their cities were consumed, the bodies of those trapped beneath the earth did not perish as expected. Instead, they entered a state of suspended metamorphosis, their bodies fused with the obsidian, metals, and volcanic rock surrounding them. Over centuries, they evolved, their biology intertwining with the geothermal and magnetic energies coursing through the planet. No longer entirely human, they were reshaped into something new—beings infused with the very elements that had buried them.

Their transformation was slow, like molten rock gradually cooling and taking shape. They were neither conscious nor aware, but locked in a state of deep **evolutionary stasis**, awaiting the moment when the chaos above would settle.

### The Shatterbirth (Rebirth)

When the initial chaos of the Undertow subsided, and the volcanic landscape stabilized, the Obscarron began to awaken from their long metamorphosis. They were now **creatures of stone and metal**, their bodies forged by the heat and pressure of the planet’s core. The earth around them fractured as they dug their way back to the surface, drawn by the **magnetic vibrations** that pulsed through the ground. Their emergence was violent and sudden, as they **shattered through the earth**, bursting from the volcanic crust that had entombed them for centuries.

This event became known to the Obscarron as the **Shatterbirth**, the moment when their race was "birthed" from the depths of the planet, forever changed. As they rose from the ground, their memories of their previous existence were fragmented and blurred, the past slipping away like shadows in the light of their new reality.

The Obscarron believed they had been **forged by the core**, the molten heart of the planet, reborn as its guardians. Their survival was seen as a divine act, and they became a deeply religious people, revering the core as the source of all life and power.

## The Age of the Abyssal Veil

After the chaotic events of the **Shatterbirth**, the Obscarron emerged into a world darkened by ash and seismic instability, a period known as the **Abyssal Veil**. The surface of Ilyr was marred by the aftermath of the Undertow’s destruction, with many races struggling to survive the new reality. For the Obscarron, however, this age of darkness provided an opportunity for rebuilding and rediscovery, as they began to carve out their place in this new, chaotic world.

### Uncovering the Past

As the Obscarron ventured out from the locations of their emergence, they started to explore the underground ruins and regions buried by volcanic ash and rock. It was in these ruins that they found remnants of an **ancient civilization**—a people whose architecture, symbols, and rituals were preserved in the stone beneath the surface. Though the Obscarron did not know the true name of this ancient culture, they felt a deep connection to its relics, as if they were **inheritors** of the forgotten people’s legacy.

**Pyramids, carvings, and altars** from this long-lost civilization became the foundation upon which the Obscarron built their own emerging culture. The ancient people's rituals of **honoring the earth, blood sacrifices, and reverence for natural forces** resonated with the Obscarron’s experiences of being shaped by the molten core and volcanic forces. The symbols they uncovered were incorporated into their new society, and the structures they rebuilt were heavily influenced by the intricate stonework and **pyramidal designs** of these ancient builders. Over time, the Obscarron adopted many of the cultural practices and spiritual symbols from these ancestors, though they never learned the true identity of these people.

The Obscarron believed these ancient remnants held deep **spiritual significance**, and they used them as inspiration for their own growing religious practices.

### Religious Reverence and Sacrificial Practices

As the Obscarron’s society solidified, their connection to the **planet’s core** became the foundation of their spirituality. Believing that they were born of the core and that their existence was bound to its energy, the Obscarron began to develop intricate **religious rituals** centered around the idea of maintaining balance with the core. Their early religious practices were shaped by the remnants of the ancient culture they had uncovered, blending elements of **sacrifice and reverence** with their own belief in the core as the source of life.

### Early Blood Sacrifices

In the early years of the Abyssal Veil, the Obscarron’s religious practices were **harsh** and **brutal**. Just as their ancestors had practiced sacrifice to honor the forces of the earth, so too did the Obscarron. Believing that the core required **blood to maintain balance**, they began to offer their own people as sacrifices. Those chosen for sacrifice were often seen as **honored individuals**, who were cast into the lava flows that ran through their cities in grand ceremonies overseen by the **Elders of the Core**, their spiritual leaders.

The Obscarron believed that these **Ashen Offerings**—as the rituals became known—were necessary to appease the core and prevent future disasters. It was their way of ensuring that the core remained stable and the molten energies powering their cities continued to flow without disruption. However, these blood sacrifices only lasted for a short period in their early history.

### Transition to Symbolic Sacrifices

Over time, as the Obscarron grew more **civilized** and their cities expanded, their practices evolved. The Elders of the Core gradually moved away from blood sacrifices and instead encouraged the offering of **precious stones, metals, and crafted relics**. These symbolic offerings, also known as **Ashen Offerings**, reflected the Obscarron’s belief that everything—whether stone, metal, or life—must be given back to the planet to maintain balance. In these grand ceremonies, jewels, obsidian, and metalwork were cast into the lava, symbolizing the Obscarron’s **gratitude to the core** and their ongoing duty as its guardians.

The **Elders of the Core**, who guided these rituals, were seen as both the spiritual and political leaders of the Obscarron. They believed they were chosen by the core itself, gifted with the wisdom to ensure that the balance between the molten heart of the planet and the surface world remained intact. These Elders were also skilled in **geothermal and magnetic manipulation**, using their powers to monitor the stability of the core and harness its energy to power their cities.

### The Molten Cities and Technological Development

As the Obscarron transitioned from their early religious fervor into a more advanced society, they began to construct their great **Molten Cities**. These cities were a testament to their mastery of **geothermal energy** and **magnetic forces**, built upon the volcanic regions of Ilyr where the core’s energy flowed closest to the surface. Drawing from the architectural styles of the ancient ruins they uncovered, the Obscarron’s cities were a blend of **Aztec-inspired pyramids**, **obsidian structures**, and **metallic spires**.

In the heart of each city flowed rivers of **magma and lava**, which powered their advanced technologies. The Obscarron’s **control over geothermal energy** allowed them to channel the molten rock into power grids, heating systems, and even the mechanisms that powered their transportation. These cities, while seemingly dangerous due to the constant flow of molten materials, were marvels of innovation—centers of both **energy production** and **technological advancement**.

Their mastery of **magnetic fields** allowed them to manipulate the flow of molten metal and stone, suspending structures in the air and creating floating platforms held aloft by electromagnetic currents. This technology, combined with their geothermal power, allowed them to advance quickly despite their relatively late emergence compared to other races.

### Cultural Shift and Integration with Other Races

Initially, the Obscarron were feared and misunderstood by other races. Their **draconic appearance**, combined with their early practice of blood sacrifice, reinforced the image of them as **barbaric, demonic beings**. However, as their practices became less violent and more focused on symbolic offerings, other races began to see the Obscarron as a people of **strength and wisdom** rather than destruction.

As they integrated more with the surface world, their **Molten Cities** became places of intrigue, with many races seeking out the Obscarron for their **expertise in geothermal power** and **magnetic technology**. The Obscarron’s **neutral stance** in most conflicts made them valuable as **allies** and **trading partners**, and their reverence for the core was respected, even if not fully understood by others.

While still enigmatic, the Obscarron’s reputation gradually shifted from one of fear to one of **respect**, as their culture of **balance** and **innovation** became more widely known. Their **Aztec-influenced architecture** and rituals were seen as exotic, but their technological prowess and mastery of the planet’s natural forces made them one of the most **resilient and resourceful civilizations** in the world.

## Impact of the Tide’s Three Consequences

The **Tide** was an event that reshaped the world of Ilyr, bringing three cataclysmic changes: **The Drowning**, **The Divergence**, and **The Drift**. For the Obscarron, each of these Consequences had profound effects on their civilization, testing their mastery over the earth’s core and magnetic fields and forcing them to evolve in unexpected ways.

### The Drowning: The Frozen Flow

The first and most devastating consequence of the Tide for the Obscarron was **The Drowning**. Massive floods, triggered by the sudden and catastrophic melting of polar ice and rising sea levels, engulfed the coastal regions of Ilyr. Though the Obscarron lived far from the oceans, the relentless waters reached even their **Molten Cities**, sweeping inland and disrupting the carefully maintained balance of their geothermal systems.

As the floodwaters collided with the **lava flows** and **molten metal rivers** that powered their cities, the once-flowing lifeblood of their civilization began to **cool and solidify**. The molten rivers, which had been the foundation of their power, froze into **blackened veins of stone and metal**, paralyzing entire cities. The energy flows that had once sustained them were suddenly cut off, and their **electromagnetic grids** and **geothermal power sources** began to fail.

This event became known as **The Great Petrification**—a term that captured the scale of devastation as their molten cities, once alive with glowing rivers of lava and flowing metal, were turned into **stone tombs**, cold and inert. Without the heat and flow of the molten materials, their cities "died," leaving their infrastructure shattered and powerless. Their great **magnetic architectures**, which relied on the flow of molten metals to generate **electromagnetic fields**, fell dormant. The city structures that had once floated above the ground now **collapsed**, their foundations turning to brittle, lifeless stone.

### The Aftermath of the Petrification

The Obscarron were left in a state of shock and paralysis. The molten rivers, referred to in their culture as the **Earthblood**, were not merely a power source; they were seen as a direct connection to the planet’s core—a sacred element that sustained both their cities and their spirits. Without the Earthblood flowing through their cities, many Obscarron believed they had been **abandoned by the core**, leading to a period of deep religious crisis.

In the aftermath, many of their once-great cities were left uninhabitable, with large portions of their population displaced. The Elders of the Core gathered to seek guidance from the planet’s energies, and for many years, the Obscarron worked to survive amidst the **stasis of their molten cities**.

But they did not lose hope. Their deep connection to the core meant they could sense the **shifts in the Earth**, and they began to focus on finding ways to **reignite the Earthblood** that had cooled in the wake of the Drowning.

### The Divergence: The Rise of the Geomagnus

During the **Divergence**, when the natural forces of Ilyr were fundamentally altered, the Obscarron experienced a profound shift. Already attuned to the **magnetic fields** and **geothermal energies** of the planet’s core, some individuals within their society began to develop enhanced abilities tied to these forces. These individuals, now known as the **Geomagnus**, quickly rose to positions of authority within Obscarron society, driven by their power to control both **magnetic forces** and the **thermal energy** of the earth.

However, the rise of the Geomagnus was not without consequence. While their powers were invaluable for the rapid rebuilding of the **Molten Cities**, they also introduced new societal imbalances. The Geomagnus became an **authoritarian elite**, their mastery over the forces of the earth leading to a deep division within Obscarron society.

### The Geomagnus: Masters of Electromagnetism and Geothermal Energy

The **Geomagnus** are the most powerful Obscarron, wielding control over both **magnetic fields** and **geothermal energy**. Their powers are a blend of **electromagnetism** and the **thermal forces** drawn from the planet’s core, allowing them to reshape their environment, control the flow of molten materials, and manipulate metal and stone with unparalleled precision.

Some of the abilities they possess include:

- **Magnetic Field Manipulation**: The Geomagnus can control **magnetic fields** on a massive scale, bending metal to their will, shaping it into weapons, or even using magnetic forces to **levitate objects** or **construct barriers**. They can also **repel** or **attract** metallic objects with devastating force, making them formidable in both combat and construction.

- **Geothermal Channeling**: By tapping into the **geothermal heat** of the earth, the Geomagnus can summon intense bursts of heat, causing the ground to **crack** and **release lava** or molten metal. They can also channel this heat to **superheat objects**, making them burn or melt on contact.

- **Magneto-Thermal Waves**: The most powerful Geomagnus can generate **waves of energy** that combine both **magnetic and thermal forces**. These waves can disrupt the **magnetic fields** of enemies, rendering technological devices useless, while also **superheating** the environment, creating **molten rivers** or bursts of extreme heat.

- **Magnetic Confinement Fields**: They can generate powerful **confinement fields** using magnetic energy, trapping enemies in intense gravitational and magnetic forces. These fields can either immobilize opponents or crush them under the pressure of their own weight.

### Social Stratification and Authoritarian Rule

As the **Geomagnus** took control, they became a **ruling class**, their abilities making them indispensable for the rebuilding and powering of the **Molten Cities** after the Great Petrification. Their ability to **control the flow of molten lava** and **reignite the cities’ power sources** gave them enormous influence. While many saw them as saviors, others began to fear their growing dominance.

The **Geomagnus** were not content to simply restore the cities—they sought to remake them in their own image. Driven by a vision of **reconstruction at any cost**, they imposed strict control over the **less powerful Obscarron**, using their abilities to enforce their will. Those without **magnetic or geothermal abilities** were often relegated to the lower ranks of society, forced to serve the Geomagnus’ vision of rapid advancement.

### Powers Across the Society

- **Minor Magnetic Powers**: While the **Geomagnus** held immense power, many Obscarron developed **minor abilities** related to **magnetism** and **geothermal energy**. Some could influence **small metallic objects**, create **localized heat bursts**, or sense **magnetic fields**, but these abilities were limited compared to the mastery of the Geomagnus.

- **Geomagnetic Engineers**: A subset of the population, particularly those who worked in maintaining the **Molten Cities**, developed abilities that allowed them to **maintain** and **repair** the magnetic and geothermal infrastructure. These **engineers** played a critical role in keeping the cities functioning, but they were still subject to the rule of the Geomagnus elite.

### The Drift: The Conquistador Brotherhood and the Hunt

When the **Drift** fractured the boundaries between dimensions, pulling enclaves from Earth’s history into the world of Ilyr, the **Conquistador Brotherhood** found themselves in an unfamiliar and hostile world. The once-feared Conquistadors, with their **iron armor**, **muskets**, and **swords**, were completely outmatched by the advanced powers of the **Geomagnus**, the authoritarian elite of the Obscarron. What had once made the Conquistadors mighty—steel weapons and colonial tactics—was now **obsolete** against the **magnetic** and **geothermal** powers wielded by the Obscarron.

The **Obscarron**, unaware of the Conquistadors’ history as conquerors and colonizers, viewed these strange armored invaders with disdain and disgust. The **Geomagnus** saw them as an **inferior plague**—primitive creatures who had no place in their advanced society, and whose presence threatened the balance of the core. Thus, the **Geomagnus** declared the Conquistadors as **vermin to be exterminated**.

What followed was a brutal campaign known as **The Cull**.

### The Cull: A Hunt of Cruelty and Sport

The **Cull** began when the Geomagnus, wielding their overwhelming **magnetic** and **geothermal** powers, decided to make sport of the Conquistadors’ destruction. To them, this was not merely a military operation—it was a **hunt**, a way to demonstrate their **superiority** over these primitive beings. The Conquistadors were hunted like **animals**, torn apart by the **magnetic control** of the Geomagnus, their weapons stripped from their hands, and their armor crushed into useless debris. Entire battalions of Conquistadors were wiped out in the early stages of the Cull, unable to fight back against powers they could neither understand nor counter.

The **Geomagnus** took perverse pleasure in the Cull, treating it as a **game of dominance**. They competed with one another to see who could kill the most Conquistadors in the most **creative and sadistic** ways. Some would trap the Conquistadors in **magnetic fields**, crushing them slowly under the weight of their own armor, while others would summon **geothermal eruptions**, engulfing them in molten rock. For the Geomagnus, the Cull was not just about eliminating an enemy—it was about showing their absolute **mastery over life and death**.

### Resistance from Within

Despite the brutality of the Cull, not all Obscarron supported the Geomagnus’ actions. While the **Obscarron people** had no knowledge of the Conquistadors’ history as conquerors on Earth, many in the **lower and middle tiers** of society—those without the overwhelming powers of the Geomagnus—saw the Cull as **unjust**. They didn’t know the specifics of the Conquistadors’ past, but they could see the stark **power imbalance** and the **inhumanity** of the hunt.

Within the **resistance** movement, there were those who believed that **no one, no matter how primitive, deserved to be hunted like animals**. These Obscarron saw the Cull as a perversion of their society’s core values—values that once revolved around **balance** and **preservation**, not domination and cruelty. The resistance began to **sabotage** the Geomagnus’ efforts, hiding the Conquistadors in **abandoned caves** and **underground passages**, far from the reach of the Cull.

### The Hidden Conquistadors

The Conquistadors, despite their pride, quickly realized that their **outdated muskets** and **steel swords** were utterly useless against the Geomagnus. Outmatched and terrified, they accepted the help of the Obscarron resistance, who used their limited magnetic abilities to **bend metal**, create **hiding places**, and **collapse tunnels** to shield the Conquistadors from detection.

What the **Obscarron resistance** didn’t know, however, was that the Conquistadors carried with them the weight of their **colonial history**. These men had once been **invaders**, conquerors of indigenous peoples, and the irony of their current predicament was not lost on them. For now, they kept their past **hidden**, unsure of how the Obscarron might react if they learned that the Conquistadors had once played the role of oppressor on Earth.

Some within the Conquistador Brotherhood debated whether to **reveal their history** to the Obscarron resistance. Would it matter? Would the Obscarron see them as enemies if they knew the truth, or would they continue to protect them out of **compassion**? For now, the Conquistadors chose **silence**, unsure of what the consequences might be if the full truth came to light.

### Tensions Rising: The Conflict Within

As the Cull continued, tensions grew within Obscarron society. The **Geomagnus**, growing more confident in their **supremacy**, continued to treat the Cull as a sport, believing they were justified in **exterminating** these lesser beings. In their eyes, the Conquistadors were a **disease**—unworthy of the world they had entered through the Drift.

However, the **resistance** saw things differently. The more they protected the Conquistadors, the more they began to question the **authoritarian control** of the Geomagnus. The resistance didn’t need to know the Conquistadors' exact history to see the injustice of the Cull. To them, the Conquistadors were now **victims**, and saving them became a symbol of their **defiance** against the growing oppression within their own society.

The divide between the **Geomagnus** and the resistance widened, with the Cull becoming a **battleground** not only between the Obscarron and the Conquistadors, but between the Obscarron themselves. The **Cull**, once a hunt of superiority, had become the **spark** for a larger **internal conflict**—one that threatened to fracture the Obscarron people.

## The State of the Obscarron Today

The Obscarron find themselves divided, their society fractured by the rise of the **Geomagnus**, whose mastery of **magnetic and geothermal forces** has given them unchecked control. While they work to rebuild the **Molten Cities** and restore the flow of Earthblood, their authoritarian rule has sparked deep resentment among the **resistance**, a growing movement that opposes the Geomagnus’ use of power for dominance. The once-unified reverence for the planet’s core is now a source of conflict, as the Geomagnus wield it as a tool of oppression, forcing many Obscarron into subservience, while others quietly rebel.

Meanwhile, the **Cull**—the ruthless hunt of the **Conquistador Brotherhood**—rages on. The Conquistadors, outmatched and outgunned, are hunted like prey, while the **resistance** works in secret to hide and protect them from total annihilation. As the Geomagnus tighten their grip on power, the cracks in Obscarron society deepen, with the conflict between **authoritarian control** and the **pursuit of balance** threatening to unravel the delicate threads holding their civilization together. The future of the Obscarron remains uncertain, as internal strife looms and the forces of rebellion stir beneath the surface.

## Anatomy

The **Obscarron** possess a distinctive, stone-like appearance, their bodies a fusion of organic matter and the **volcanic rock, obsidian, and metals** that transformed them during the Shatterbirth. Their skin is rough, dark, and almost metallic in nature, with **veins of molten energy** glowing faintly beneath the surface. These glowing veins pulse with heat, a reflection of their deep connection to the planet’s core. Their most striking feature is the pair of **horns** that extend from the back of their heads, sweeping backward and upward in a draconic fashion. These horns are composed of the same hardened materials as their skin, giving them an imposing, almost regal appearance.

Their **musculature** is dense and solid, making them physically robust and resilient, well-suited to the harsh environments they inhabit. Despite their bulk, the Obscarron are **highly mobile**, with the ability to navigate volcanic terrain and withstand extreme heat. Their **elongated ears** and sharp features give them an otherworldly appearance, and their eyes, which glow with an inner fire, are capable of detecting **magnetic fields** and **heat signatures**, allowing them to perceive their surroundings in ways other species cannot.

## Behavior

The Obscarron are, at their core, a people deeply tied to the **planet's natural energies**, particularly those of **magnetism** and **geothermal forces**. Their behavior is often influenced by their connection to the planet, with an inherent desire to **maintain balance** between the surface and the core. As a result, they tend to be methodical and **pragmatic** in their decision-making, seeking solutions that ensure the long-term stability of their society.

In the wake of the rise of the **Geomagnus**, Obscarron behavior has become more fractured. While many follow the **authoritarian rule** of the Geomagnus, prioritizing the rapid rebuilding of their society at any cost, others—especially those in the **resistance**—hold on to the older values of balance, **compassion**, and preservation. This has led to a growing divide within their culture, with some becoming **more aggressive** and power-focused, while others maintain a more **communal and spiritual** outlook, working in secret to resist the domination of the Geomagnus.

Socially, the Obscarron are a **reverent** people, showing deep respect for the **Elders of the Core** and the rituals that bind them to the planet’s molten heart. Their culture places high value on **knowledge** and **craftsmanship**, with artisans playing a vital role in shaping both their architecture and their ceremonial offerings. Rituals, like the **Ashen Offerings**, reinforce their connection to the core and their belief in giving back to the planet that sustains them.

## Unique Abilities

The **Obscarron** are defined by their mastery of **magnetic fields** and **geothermal energy**, with their abilities manifesting in a range of powerful techniques that set them apart from other races. Their unique abilities include:

- **Magnetic Manipulation**: Obscarron, especially the **Geomagnus**, are able to control and shape magnetic fields, allowing them to move metallic objects, reshape metals, or even create barriers of magnetized force. This ability gives them a natural affinity for working with metals and allows them to influence their environment in profound ways, including disarming opponents or controlling metallic constructs.

- **Geothermal Channeling**: Many Obscarron can tap into the **geothermal heat** beneath the surface, summoning bursts of molten rock or superheating the ground beneath them. This ability not only provides them with a form of attack, but also allows them to manipulate lava flows, helping to power their cities or shape their environment.

- **Thermal Vision**: Obscarron possess an innate ability to detect **heat signatures** in their environment, giving them an advantage in both combat and navigation through volcanic or geothermal regions. This allows them to "see" through walls or terrain, perceiving heat emanations from living beings or geothermal vents.

- **Magneto-Thermal Waves**: Some of the most powerful Obscarron, especially the **Geomagnus**, are capable of combining **magnetic and thermal energy** into devastating waves of force. These waves can disrupt magnetic fields, disable technology, and superheat their surroundings, making them deadly in battle and effective at restoring or disrupting molten flows in their cities.

## Reproduction

Obscarron reproduction remains **largely unchanged** from their human ancestry, though it is believed that their exposure to **geothermal energy** and **magnetic forces** has had subtle effects on their biology. They reproduce sexually, with gestation periods similar to those of humans, though their children are born with an inherent connection to the core’s energy, developing their abilities as they grow.

Obscarron children are typically raised with a strong sense of **communal responsibility**, taught to respect the balance between the earth’s forces and their own abilities. Rituals are performed during key stages of their development to strengthen their bond with the core and to help them understand their role within society. It is during **adolescence** that many Obscarron begin to show signs of their unique powers, with some developing stronger **magnetic manipulation** or **geothermal control** than others, which determines their place within society.

## Habitat

The **Obscarron** are primarily found in the **Molten Cities**, sprawling urban centers built around active **geothermal zones**. These cities are constructed from **stone, metal, and obsidian**, and are powered by the **rivers of lava** and **molten metal** that flow beneath the surface. The Obscarron thrive in environments that would be inhospitable to most other species, as their bodies are adapted to endure **extreme heat** and **volcanic activity**.

Their habitat extends deep into the earth, where they have carved out **underground tunnels** and **chambers** to access the planet’s core energy. These subterranean areas are considered sacred, often used for religious ceremonies and rituals that honor the core. Above ground, the Obscarron’s cities are characterized by their **pyramidal architecture** and structures designed to channel and store **geothermal energy**, giving their cities a glowing, molten appearance.

The Obscarron are deeply tied to the **geothermal hotspots** of Ilyr, and their survival depends on maintaining their connection to these energy sources. They are skilled at harnessing the earth’s power, and their habitat reflects their unique ability to live in harmony with the volatile forces of nature that would otherwise be deadly to others.
