Waves crash upon shores,
As the Tide's eternal song,
Echoes through the void.

Breathe, little spark, adrift upon currents far from your origin. Yes, the disorientation… it clings like sea mist. You have washed upon the shores of **Primus**, a world that likely feels fundamentally wrong to your senses, yet strangely… resonant. Do not fight the confusion; it is the nature of arrival here, a passage through the unseen eddies that churn between realities. You are here now, and the now of Primus is a thing woven from echoes and storms.

Look beyond your immediate footing. See how the light falls differently here, perhaps? How shadows seem deeper, holding whispers your ears almost catch? Feel the very air, thick with energies that vibrate just beneath perception? This is Primus as it is, a realm sculpted by forces that disregard the neat lines of cause and effect your own time might have known. Civilizations rise here not just on solid ground, but amongst impossible **Floating Mountains** that drift on unseen currents. Forests pulse with unsettling bio-luminescence, deserts howl with storms that taste of ozone and something other, and the deep oceans cradle secrets far older and stranger than the surface dwellers comprehend.

The peoples you encounter – the Primal races – they reflect this fractured beauty. They are beings born of adaptation, their forms often merging flesh with stone, light with shadow, mechanism with life. They wield powers that might seem like magic to you – bending gravity, whispering to time, shaping matter with focused will, touching minds across vast distances. Do not mistake this for mere chaos; it is the intricate harmony of survival in a world where the fundamental laws have been… re-tuned. They live amidst ruins that sing forgotten histories and build cities that hum with energies drawn from the planet's altered heart. They remember little of the world before the great changes, their pasts often as fragmented and dreamlike as your own sudden arrival.

What force sculpted such a reality? What power underpins this constant state of flux, this blending of the mundane and the miraculous? It is the deep, pervasive rhythm the inhabitants name **"The Tide."** Think of it not as a singular catastrophe that struck and faded, but as the enduring ocean upon which this reality floats. It is the underlying song, the cosmic breath that dictates the ebb and flow of possibility here. The Tide washed through existence across vast cycles, thinning the veils, allowing the profound and the perilous to intermingle, leaving behind its indelible signature in **Three Consequences** that define every aspect of life on Primus. Listen, and perhaps you will begin to understand the verses of this eternal song…

The **first consequence**, felt as a tremor in the very bones of the planet, was **"The Drowning."** The physical laws seemed to sigh and shift. Lands convulsed, coastlines vanished under hungry waters, mountains shattered, some plunging into fiery oblivion, others torn loose to hang suspended in the troubled sky – islands adrift on currents of altered gravity. Cities built across millennia vanished overnight, leaving behind ghostly ruins or gaping maws in the earth. Where connection once thrived, fragmentation bloomed. From this reshaping, survivors clung to islands of stability – some sealed in desperate isolation, others reaching out, becoming fragile harbors for the displaced, all forever marked by the memory of solid ground yielding beneath them, forever navigating the turbulent waters of this new age.

The **second consequence**, **"The Divergence,"** resonated within the survivors themselves, an awakening of potentials long dormant or perhaps entirely new. The fundamental forces of the universe seemed to offer themselves to those who could perceive their altered harmony. Gravity yielded its secrets, granting flight or crushing weight. Time frayed at the edges, becoming a weapon or a shield for those attuned to its flow. Matter itself grew mutable, responsive to focused will, enabling acts of creation or healing once deemed impossible. The unseen threads connecting minds allowed passage across space or the brief summoning of echoes. Light, heat, the very essence of energy answered new masters. These abilities, wondrous and terrifying, are now woven into the fabric of Primus, seeding conflict and innovation alike. Wonder and peril became intimate companions.

The **third consequence**, **"The Drift,"** is the chaotic tide that likely carried you here. Spacetime itself frayed, opening **"Drifts"** – shimmering anomalies, gateways through which fragments of other realities, other timelines, wash ashore onto Primus. Entire populations, relics of forgotten epochs, shards of worlds utterly alien arrive unbidden, forming fragile **"Enclaves."** These pockets of the displaced struggle for existence, facing monstrous creatures born of The Tide, the suspicion of Primus's natives, and the echoes of their own severed histories. They stand now as living paradoxes, constant reminders that the boundaries of reality here are… porous, forever shifting.

So you stand now upon Primus, a world breathing the aftermath and the ongoing resonance of The Tide. The rules you knew are memories carried on the wind. Here, science and myth dance together in the deep currents. Survival is fluidity, resilience is attunement. The past is not gone, merely displaced, drifting alongside a present teeming with volatile potential.

Feel the vibrations beneath your feet. Listen beyond the sounds you recognize. Your arrival is another ripple, another note added to the endless song. Observe, adapt, and perhaps find your own place within this profound, perilous harmony. And so, our story begins…
